FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Does money go directly to my bank account? +
Yes, absolutely. Your Razorpay or Paytm account is connected to your own bank account. When a customer pays, the money goes directly to your account. NetFlow does not handle or receive any payments — we only provide the software platform.
Which ISP servers do you support? +
We currently support Jaze Server and NetAdmin/Mikrotik servers. We are actively working on integrations with other bandwidth management platforms. Contact us if you need a specific integration.
How long does setup take? +
Most accpunts are fully set up within 24-48 hours. This includes configuring your NetAdmin panel, adding your customers, setting up payment integration, and sharing the NetFlow MyWiFi app with your customers. We handle everything for you.
Can I manage multiple franchises or branches? +
Yes! The Growth and Enterprise plans include full multi-franchise support. Each franchise gets their own NetAdmin panel, customer database, payment account, and credentials — fully isolated. As the super admin, you can see all franchises from one dashboard.
Is my customers' data and my API keys secure? +
Yes. All data is stored with enterprise-grade encryption. Payment API keys (Razorpay/Paytm) and ISP credentials (Jaze/NetAdmin) are encrypted on server no one can access them.
What happens if a customer's payment fails? +
Failed payments are tracked in the NetAdmin dashboard. The customer's account is not renewed and they continue to receive expiry notifications. The customer can retry the payment at any time from the NetFlow MyWiFi app.
What is your refund/cancellation policy? +
You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. We offer a 7-day refund for the first subscription payment if you are not satisfied. See our full Refund Policy for details.

All data is stored with industry-standard encryption. Payment API keys and other credentials are accessible only to the designated super admin of each franchise. We use HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit. Access to production systems is strictly controlled.
