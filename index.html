FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does money go directly to my bank account? +
Yes, absolutely. Your own Easebuzz account is connected to your bank account. When a customer pays, the money goes directly to your account. NetFlow Cloud does not handle or receive your customers' payments — we only provide the software platform.
Which ISP servers do you support? +
We currently support Jaze Server and NetAdmin/Mikrotik servers. We are actively working on integrations with other bandwidth management platforms. Contact us if you need a specific integration.
How long does setup take? +
Most accounts are fully set up within 24-48 hours. This includes configuring your NetAdmin panel, adding your customers, setting up payment integration, and sharing the NetFlow MyWiFi app with your customers.
Can I manage multiple franchises or branches? +
Yes! NetFlow Cloud supports multiple franchises and branches. Each franchise gets their own NetAdmin panel, customer database, payment account, and credentials — fully isolated.
Is my customers' data and my API keys secure? +
Yes. All data is stored with enterprise-grade encryption. Payment API keys (Easebuzz) and ISP credentials (Jaze/NetAdmin) are encrypted on server — no one can access them.
What is your refund/cancellation policy? +
You can cancel at any time. We offer a 7-day refund for the first subscription payment if you're not satisfied. See our full Refund Policy for details.
Does NetFlow Cloud send WhatsApp reminders to customers? +
Yes. NetFlow Cloud connects to the official WhatsApp Business API to send payment receipts, expiry reminders, plan-expired alerts and welcome messages automatically. WhatsApp message credits are purchased separately.
Does NetFlow Cloud support prepaid and postpaid billing? +
Yes. You can bill each customer prepaid or postpaid, set grace periods, auto-suspend overdue accounts, accept partial payments and keep a full bill history for every customer.
Can I manage IPTV with NetFlow Cloud? +
Yes. NetFlow Cloud includes a separate IPTV portal with per-device billing, so you can manage IPTV customers and devices alongside your broadband customers. IPTV device slots are purchased separately.
