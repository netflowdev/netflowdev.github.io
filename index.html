FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Does money go directly to my bank account? +
Yes, absolutely. Your Razorpay or Paytm account is connected to your own bank account. When a customer pays, the money goes directly to your account. NetFlow does not handle or receive any payments — we only provide the software platform.
Which ISP servers do you support? +
We currently support Jaze Server and NetAdmin/Mikrotik servers. We are actively working on integrations with other bandwidth management platforms. Contact us if you need a specific integration.
How long does setup take? +
Most accounts are fully set up within 24-48 hours. This includes configuring your NetAdmin panel, adding your customers, setting up payment integration, and sharing the NetFlow MyWiFi app with your customers.
Can I manage multiple franchises or branches? +
Yes! The Growth and Enterprise plans include full multi-franchise support. Each franchise gets their own NetAdmin panel, customer database, payment account, and credentials — fully isolated.
Is my customers' data and my API keys secure? +
Yes. All data is stored with enterprise-grade encryption. Payment API keys (Razorpay/Paytm) and ISP credentials (Jaze/NetAdmin) are encrypted on server — no one can access them.
What is your refund/cancellation policy? +
You can cancel at any time. We offer a 7-day refund for the first subscription payment if you're not satisfied. See our full Refund Policy for details.

1. Introduction

NetFlow operates the NetFlow platform, including the NetAdmin web dashboard and the NetFlow MyWiFi Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information of ISP operators and their end customers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Operator Account Data: Name, email, mobile, business name, address, server credentials, and payment gateway API keys.
  • Customer Data: Broadband customer names, mobile numbers, plan details, expiry dates, and payment history as entered by Operators.
  • Transaction Data: Payment order IDs, transaction IDs, amounts, and payment status records.
  • Usage Data: Log data including IP addresses, device type, and pages visited.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the NetFlow platform.
  • To process subscription payments.
  • To configure integrations and trigger automated activations.
  • To send transactional notifications.
  • To respond to support queries.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with payment gateways (Razorpay/Paytm), ISP servers (Jaze/NetAdmin), and cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare) as needed to operate the service.

5. Data Security

All data is stored with industry-standard encryption. Payment API keys are accessible only to designated super admins. We use HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit.
